## ⚠️ Important Fitment Clarification: ETRTO Rim Tables vs. Real Chassis Fitment

A tyre manufacturer’s approved rim-width range (e.g., 7.5J–9.5J) **only confirms that the tyre bead will seat safely on the rim**. It does **not** guarantee physical vehicle fitment.

An approved rim table does **not** verify:
1. Inner sidewall clearance from suspension struts and control arms.
2. Outer sidewall clearance from fender lips and wheel arch liners under compression.
3. Full steering lock clearance (zero scrubbing on inner liners).
4. Steering and suspension geometry retention.
5. Tyre tread squirm caused by rim pinching.

## 🏆 Real-World Option Evaluation & Ranking for Stock 8Jx19 Wheels

```
Stock Profile (245/55 R19):   |====================|  245 mm
🥇 Option 1 (265/50 R19):     |======================|  265 mm (+20 mm)
🥈 Option 2 (255/55 R19):     |=====================|   255 mm (+10 mm)
🥉 Option 3 (275/50 R19):     |========================|  275 mm (+30 mm)
```

### Option 1: **265/50 R19** (The Sweet Spot)
* **Overall Diameter:** `747.6 mm` (**-0.60%** / **-2.25 mm** radius).
* **Section Width:** `265 mm` (**+20 mm** nominal / **+10 mm** per side).
* **Fitment Reality:** Excellent balance. Keeps speedometer reading nearly identical to factory specs while offering a noticeably wider profile. Highly likely to clear struts and fender liners on the stock **8Jx19** rim. The lower profile slightly reduces sidewall flex for better cornering stability.

### Option 2: **255/55 R19** (The Conservative Pick)
* **Overall Diameter:** `763.1 mm` (**+1.46%** / **+5.50 mm** radius).
* **Section Width:** `255 mm` (**+10 mm** nominal / **+5 mm** per side).
* **Fitment Reality:** The safest choice for retaining factory driving characteristics, suspension geometry, and ride comfort. Fills the wheel arch slightly more without straining the 8J rim width.

### Option 3: **275/50 R19** (Exercise Caution)
* **Overall Diameter:** `757.6 mm` (**+0.73%** / **+2.75 mm** radius).
* **Section Width:** `275 mm` (**+30 mm** nominal / **+15 mm** per side).
* **Fitment Reality:** While the mathematical diameter error is small (+0.73%), mounting a 275 mm section width on an 8J rim pinched the tire sidewalls, potentially compromising tread stability and turn-in sharpness. Requires physical verification for inner strut clearance and full lock steering scrub.

## ⚡ Load Index, Tyre Inflation & Wheel Dynamics

1. **Load Index Matching:**
   * Replacement tyres **must meet or exceed** the OEM load index specified on your vehicle's B-pillar placard. Do not rely on blanket generic thresholds.
   * Due to heavy EV battery weight, **XL (Extra Load)** tyres are strongly recommended.

2. **Tyre Pressure Adjustments:**
   * Do not simply copy the factory 36 PSI rating when changing tyre dimensions or load indices.
   * Determine optimal cold inflation pressure by referencing ETRTO/TRA load-vs-inflation tables against your BE 6's Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR).

3. **Wider Tyres vs. Wider Wheels:**
   * Mounting wider tyres on the stock `8Jx19` wheel (e.g., 265/50 on 8J) is not identical to running a wider wheel (e.g., 265/50 on a `9Jx19` wheel).
   * A 9J wheel flattens the sidewall, reducing flex and improving turn-in response. When upgrading wheels, **wheel offset (ET)** is the critical parameter for inner and outer clearance.

## 🔍 Pre-Purchase Verification Checklist

To calculate exact inner clearance, outer poke, and scrub radius before purchasing:
1. Locate the offset marking stamped on the back of your stock 19" wheels (e.g., `8Jx19 ET45`).
2. Provide your factory wheel **ET (Offset)**, **PCD**, and **Center Bore** to run precise clearance models for your specific setup.
